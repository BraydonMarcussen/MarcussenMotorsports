About the store

Metrik is a customizable digital automotive gauge from Marcussen Motorsports, based in Iowa, United States. These terms cover this website and future direct purchases from the store.

The store is being prepared for launch. Product pricing, availability, delivery terms, and the seller’s final contact details will be provided before orders are accepted.

02

Product information

Review the product description and compatibility information before buying. The current gauge specifications include a 52 mm body diameter, 56 mm bezel outer diameter, 7 mm bezel depth, and 17 mm depth behind the bezel. The display is rated at 450 nits and has an anti-glare coating.

Animations, app recordings and example screen profiles illustrate the product. A phone shown in a demonstration is not a promise that a phone is included. Included accessories, supported features, and app availability will be stated in the product listing.

Do not assume compatibility with every vehicle, sensor, or data source. Follow the supplied installation instructions and confirm the required electrical connections before installation. Configure and interact with the gauge only when it is safe to do so.

CE conformity and FCC authorization are launch goals. This preview does not claim that either process has been completed; market availability will be confirmed after the relevant requirements are addressed.

Orders and payment

Before submitting an order, check the product, quantity, currency, delivery address, shipping charges, taxes, and total shown at checkout. Use accurate contact details so we can send purchase and delivery information.

Checkout is intended to be hosted by Stripe. Available payment methods will be shown there. Payment processing by Stripe does not make Stripe the seller of the gauge; Marcussen Motorsports handles the product, fulfillment, and customer support.

An item will be clearly identified as a preorder before payment if it is not ready to ship. We will not substitute a product or charge a revised price without your agreement. If we cannot fulfill an order, we will contact you and refund any payment that must be returned.
